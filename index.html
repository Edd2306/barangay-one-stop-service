Our Community

Barangay Parang is a vibrant and growing community in Marikina City, built on unity, cooperation, and public service. Together, residents and local leaders work toward a safe, inclusive, and progressive community.

Our portal makes essential barangay services and information more accessible and convenient for everyone.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To provide accessible, efficient, and reliable barangay services while promoting responsive governance, community participation, and the overall welfare of the residents of Barangay Parang.

Vision

A progressive, safe, and digitally connected Barangay Parang where residents have convenient access to quality public services and actively participate in building a stronger community.

ABOUT BARANGAY PARANG
🏘️

About Our Community

Barangay Parang is one of the established communities in Marikina City, Metro Manila. Its history is closely connected to the development and growth of Marikina as a city. Over the years, Barangay Parang has grown from a developing residential community into a vibrant and progressive neighborhood, with its population and local establishments continuing to expand.

As the community developed, the barangay continued to strengthen its public services and programs to address the needs of its residents. Today, Barangay Parang remains committed to promoting community welfare, maintaining peace and order, supporting local development, and providing accessible services to its constituents.

The Barangay One-Stop Service Portal represents a step toward a more convenient and modern way of serving the community, allowing residents to access essential barangay services, submit requests, book appointments, and receive important information online.
